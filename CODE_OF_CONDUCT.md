# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct, v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, focus on the code. Disagreements
about technical direction are normal and welcome; personal attacks,
harassment, or sustained disruption are not.

Reports of unacceptable behavior may be sent to hi@mentiora.com.
Reports are handled confidentially and acknowledged within 72 hours.
